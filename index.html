    Recommendation tool

    What should I recommend?

    Ask two or three questions, not all of them. The goal is a conversation that ends in one confident recommendation. Answer what you know and skip the rest.

    1. How experienced are they with cigars?
    2. What do they usually drink?
    3. Strong flavors, like black coffee or spicy food, or something smoother?
    4. What's their budget today?

    Open the Humidor Tracker

    Safety when alone: keep your phone with you, keep the doors in your sight, don't announce cash totals, and call your manager or 911 if anything feels off. Fire safety is serious in a cigar shop: never leave a lit torch or cigar unattended, and double-check the ashtrays.

    Learn our humidor

    Humidor Tracker

    Knowing exactly what's on your shelves is the biggest edge you can have. Fill this in during training and add rows as you learn. Aim for two or three cigars per body level (mild, medium, full) that you can recommend with confidence.

    Check with your manager. Ask for the price list, the house blend details, and which cigars are limited, allocated, or one per customer.

    People

    Regulars Tracker

    People come back to places where they're known. After each visit, jot a line or two.

    Privacy. Keep only what the customer would be comfortable with you remembering: names, favorite cigars, drinks, and an easy conversation topic. Don't write down anything sensitive or personal beyond that. These notes stay in this browser on this device.

    Habits that build regulars

    • Use their name the second time you see them.
    • Say “the usual?” when you know it, and offer a new one if they're open.
    • Follow up on what they tried: “How did you like the Liga Privada?”
    • Mention new arrivals that fit their taste.
    • Thank them and say “see you next time” as they leave.

    Hands-on

    Tasting Journal

    Smoke something new each week and write it down. Note what changes across the three thirds: the introduction, the heart, and the finish.
